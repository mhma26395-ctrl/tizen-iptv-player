## Tizen IPTV Player v1.0

The first release of Tizen IPTV Player, a player application designed for compatible Samsung Tizen Smart TVs.

### Features
- IPTV player functionality.
- No built-in channels or playlists.
- Users are responsible for providing and managing their own playlists.
- Designed for compatible Samsung Tizen TV models.

### Important Information
- This application is a standalone project developed independently.
- It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially associated with Samsung Electronics.
- The application does not provide, host, or distribute any TV channels or media content.
- Users are solely responsible for the playlists and content they choose to access, and must ensure they have the necessary rights and permissions.
- Compatibility may vary depending on the TV model, Tizen version, and device specifications. Please verify compatibility before installing.
